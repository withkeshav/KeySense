## Limits of results

A valid phrase, matching address or passing test does not prove secrecy, ownership,
a balance, safety or compatibility with every wallet. KeySense does not check live
balances or send transactions. Recovery helpers search only their stated candidates.
They cannot promise recovery, and no match does not prove that funds are absent.
Brain-wallet and vanity exercises do not establish a safe way to hold funds.
Course completion is not a professional qualification or proof of expertise.

## Printing and backups

Paper output exposes seed words and private keys to the screen, browser and print
system. Copies may remain in PDFs, printer storage, print queues, clipboard history
or backups. The address QR code does not contain the secret, but the rest of the
sheet does. A separate BIP39 passphrase is not printed. Keep required wallet policy
and recovery information in mind; a sheet is not a complete backup for every wallet.
Do not fund examples or treat this print feature as certified cold storage.

## Your checks and responsibility

Check the complete destination, network, asset and wallet settings independently
before any real use of derived information. Confirm that recovery works with
appropriate test data. A compromised host, extension, browser, device or printer
can defeat application protections. Offline use reduces network exposure but does
not make compromised equipment trustworthy. Crypto transfers may be irreversible.
