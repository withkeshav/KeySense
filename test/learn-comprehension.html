Before the reader starts: run the check on the current page

The reader must not have seen the app's Learn tab yet. Ask each question out loud, let them answer, type their words. Do not explain, prompt, or correct. If they say they do not know, write that.

Then: three tasks, no help, no hints

Give the reader the app. Say: take as long as you want, and you cannot do it wrong. Watch, do not teach. Record which task they complete alone.

Task 1: in the Learn tab, find and read the first step, then say back what the seed phrase is and why the last word matters. Task 2: on the Derive tab, use the Learn task buttons to load the Legacy and Native SegWit paths, then explain why one seed gave two different addresses. Task 3: say one thing that would make a seed weak, and one thing you must never do with a seed holding real money.

"Done alone" means it happened with no help from you and no more than the page itself. Record the nudges verbatim in the notes box, they are the most useful output of the whole session.

Scoring, for the facilitator only. Post 1 is correct if the reader says the last word contains or is checked against a checksum, so a mistyped phrase is rejected instead of silently becoming a different wallet. Post 2 is correct if they say the derivation path changed, or the account or index changed, or the wallet picked a different default path. Post 4 is correct if they say never type it into a website, never share it, never photograph it, or never store it online. Post 3 is correct if they name weak input, for example words a person chose, a quote, or a passphrase without enough randomness. Pass bar for the page as a whole: 4 of 5 readers complete tasks 1 and 2 alone, and 4 of 5 answer Post 1 and Post 2 correctly. Below that, the lesson did not land and the content is what changes, not the reader.
